Help — Spectrum navigation
  • Pan: press the left mouse button and drag the spectrum.
  • Zoom m/z (x-axis): place the cursor below the x-axis and scroll the mouse wheel.
  • Zoom intensity (y-axis): place the cursor above the x-axis and scroll the mouse wheel.
  • Reset: click Reset to restore the default view.
  • Envelopes of one mass: click a theoretical-peak circle to show only the circles of the envelopes with the same mass; click anywhere else in the spectrum to show all circles again.
  • One envelope: click a Ref m/z link in a mass list to center the spectrum on it and show only the circles of that envelope; click in the spectrum to show all circles again.
  • Mass lists: click a column header to sort. Group by mass (MS1) keeps the envelopes with the same mono mass together: the groups follow the sort order of their first row. Advanced sort sorts by a first and a second column.
