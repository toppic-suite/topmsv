Mouse

  • Left drag: pan the window
  • Right drag: rotate; a top-down view switches to a flat 2D map
  • Wheel inside the plot: zoom both axes; over the m/z or RT margin: zoom that axis
  • Ctrl + wheel: scale peak heights (kept while zooming and panning; Reset restores the default)
  • Ctrl + click: highlight the nearest MS1 scan

Peaks are read at the resolution level matching the window; at most 4000 of the strongest peaks are shown.
